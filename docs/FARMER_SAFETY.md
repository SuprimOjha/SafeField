# Fertilizer handling: general safety checklist

This is general precautionary information, not a substitute for the product label, safety data sheet, local regulation or professional advice.

- Wear suitable gloves and closed footwear when handling fertilizer.
- Wear long sleeves and long trousers; use a suitable dust mask where dusty material may be inhaled.
- Avoid creating or breathing dust. Handle products carefully and follow label instructions.
- Wash hands and exposed skin after handling; change contaminated clothing.
- Keep fertilizer in its original labelled container, sealed and away from children, food, animal feed and drinking water.
- Do not mix products unless the label or a qualified adviser explicitly permits it.
- Keep products dry and stored as directed by the manufacturer.
- If exposure or illness occurs, seek medical advice and take the product label or safety data sheet.

The application interface does not certify that any product, dose or application time is safe.
