# Nepal farmer-health evidence status

**No Nepal-specific study citations have been verified in this repository yet.**

The judge review mentions three Nepal studies and teammate-sourced handling points, but their bibliographic details and findings were not included in the repository files inspected for this change. They are therefore not reproduced or presented as evidence here.

## Evidence card acceptance criteria
For each proposed local evidence card, add:
- Full title and author list
- Journal/report/publisher and year
- DOI or stable public URL
- Study location and sample size
- Study design and population
- Exact finding being summarized, with page/table reference
- Limitations, including whether the study is small or observational

Do not invent study titles, sample sizes, health outcomes or causal claims. Until verified sources are added, the UI safety checklist is general precautionary guidance, not a Nepal-specific research finding.
